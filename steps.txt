1. Download the ".ppd" file & replace the "{write your bash commands here! remove the curly brackets first}" with your command. 
2. Go to Settings > Device > Print
3. Expand "Other available printers" and press "add printer manually"
4. Add a new printer with these parameters:
name: nocturne
address: 127.1.1.1
protocol: LPD
queue: ipp/print
5. Select the file you download & edited and click "Add"
6. Open Chrome, navigate to any website
7. Ctrl+P to print, change destination to "see more..." > nocturne and print, your arbitrary code will try to run. If it fails then it isn't allowed in the sandbox.
